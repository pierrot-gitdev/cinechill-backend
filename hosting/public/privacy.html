Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026 · Français

Cinechill helps you pick a film or a series. To do that, we keep what you've watched and what you like, and nothing else. We sell no data, show no ads and don't track you across apps.

Who is responsible

Cinechill is published by Pierre Robert, data controller under the GDPR. Contact: pierrerobrt@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, display name, Apple or Google sign-in identifier, your Google account photo. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never seen in clear.
  • Public profile: @handle, display name, badges.
  • Library: films and series watched, favourites, watchlist, titles skipped in Discover.
  • Taste: CinéMatch answers, the films CinéMatch showed you and the ones you picked there, streaming platforms you declare, viewing country.
  • Social: accounts you follow and that follow you, films you recommend or receive.
  • Technical: device attestation token (App Attest), IP address in server logs.

Your profile photo picked from your photo library, the app language, display preferences and the CinéMatch Live Activity stay on your phone. We don't collect your location, contacts, advertising identifier, usage analytics or crash reports.

Why

To run your account, recommend titles that fit you and are on your platforms, and run the Hall (performance of the contract, GDPR art. 6.1.b). To improve everyone's recommendations by learning which films people like together, pooling all users' libraries without ever showing yours to anyone (legitimate interest, art. 6.1.f). To protect the service from fraudulent devices and requests (legitimate interest, art. 6.1.f). No decision with legal effect is made automatically.

What other users see

Once you choose a handle, other Cinechill users can see your display name, @handle, Google account photo, badges, gallery posters and count, taste breakdown by genre, and who you follow and who follows you. Your email, watchlist, CinéMatch answers and platforms are never shown. A recommendation is visible only to its recipient.

Service providers

Google (Firebase: accounts, database, servers, fraud protection; Google Sign-In), Apple (Sign in with Apple, device attestation), TMDB (title details, posters and platforms; receives the titles you open and your IP address when your phone downloads a poster). Platform availability comes from JustWatch through TMDB; none of your data is sent to JustWatch. App servers run in Belgium (Google Cloud europe-west1). Transfers outside the EU are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. Delete it any time in Profile › Settings › Account › Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: account, gallery, watchlist, answers, public profile, handle and follow links. If you signed in with Apple, Cinechill's authorization is revoked too. Server logs, which may contain your IP address, are deleted by Google after 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, or export your data by writing to the address above, and complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local authority.

Minimum age

Cinechill is not meant for children under 15.

Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Only an Apple-attested device can call the app's servers. Your library and settings are readable by your account only.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app before a change to this policy takes effect.
